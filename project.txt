Project: MCMC Bayesian Causal Inference for Marketing Uplift

Objective:
Estimate the causal effect (uplift) of three different promotional strategies on customer conversion rates:
1. No Offer (Control Group)
2. BOGO (Buy One Get One)
3. Discount (Percentage discount)

Historical data features:
- recency: Months since the last purchase.
- history: Total historical spend in dollars.
- used_discount, used_bogo: Boolean flags indicating past usage of these promotions.
- zip_code: Categorical (Urban, Suburban, Rural).
- is_referral: Boolean flag indicating if the customer was acquired via referral.
- channel: Acquisition channel (Phone, Web, Multichannel).

Technical Requirements:
The Data Science team will use PyMC (Python) with the NUTS sampler to:
- Build a Bayesian Logistic Regression model for the conversion rate.
- Include covariate adjustment (confounders) for unbiased causal estimates.
- Vectorize operations using PyTensor.
- Extract the full posterior distribution of the causal uplift (BOGO vs Control, Discount vs Control, and Head-to-Head Discount vs BOGO).
- Simulate business ROI and cannibalization risk.
